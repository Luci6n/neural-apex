# AI Learning Systems

## Predictor — Machine Learning

Predefined historical-weather logic estimates rain with confidence. The lesson: predictions concern what may happen next.

## Pattern Scanner — Deep Learning

Scripted track and telemetry recognition reports current evidence such as a dry surface or rising tyre heat. The lesson: recognition and prediction answer different questions.

## Adaptive Driver — Reinforcement Learning

The autonomous driver changes pace, racing line, or braking behaviour after visible consequences and a plain-language team priority. The lesson: actions produce consequences that inform future actions.

## Deliberate conflict

- Predictor: rain is likely soon.
- Pattern Scanner: the circuit is still dry.
- Adaptive Driver: staying out protects position.

The player may ask why, but makes the decision.

## GPT-5.6

GPT-5.6 first synthesises the post-race prediction, detection, adaptation, setup effect, decision consequence, and lesson. It also explains conflicts on demand. Prompts contain compact deterministic setup, telemetry, event, and outcome facts. GPT never drives a car or decides outcomes. Local fallback copy preserves the same educational structure.

These are educational simulations, not trained production ML, DL, or RL models.
